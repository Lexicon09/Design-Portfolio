My design process

The steps I follow on most projects. I adjust them to fit the size of the problem, but the thinking stays the same.

01

Discover

I start with the people and the problem: talking to users and stakeholders, reviewing what already exists, and agreeing what success looks like.

  • Interviews
  • Competitor review
  • Problem statement
02

Define

I turn what I learn into a clear direction: who it is for, what they need, and what the product has to do first.

  • User flows
  • Priorities
  • Scope
03

Ideate

I sketch several routes quickly before committing to one, so the best idea wins, not the first one.

  • Sketches
  • Wireframes
  • Concepts
04

Design

I build the interface in Figma with one consistent system of type, colour and components, so the product feels like a single thing.

  • UI screens
  • Design system
  • Prototypes
05

Test & refine

I put prototypes in front of real people, watch where they hesitate, and improve the design from what I see.

  • Usability tests
  • Feedback rounds
  • Iterations
06

Build & hand off

As a design engineer I can build the front end myself, or give developers clear specs and assets, so what ships matches what I designed.

  • Front-end code
  • Specs
  • Design QA
