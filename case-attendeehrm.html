01

Overview

AttendeeHRM is a cloud-based HR platform for attendance, payroll, leave, recruitment and employee self-service, built for growing businesses. I redesigned its marketing website so visitors can understand the product quickly and book a demo or start a trial.

A business owner or HR manager lands on the site with one question: can this run our attendance, payroll and people processes? The redesign answers that quickly, then points to the two next steps that matter, booking a demo or starting a free trial.

02

The Problem

The old website made visitors work to understand what AttendeeHRM does and what to do next.

HR software is a big decision, and buyers compare several options before they ask for a demo. If a site doesn't explain the product fast, they leave for one that does.

1

The message was unclear

Visitors couldn't tell within a few seconds what the product does or who it is for.

2

Content was hard to scan

Features, benefits and pricing weren't grouped, so people had to read a lot to find what mattered to them.

3

The next step was hidden

Booking a demo or starting a trial wasn't obvious, so interested visitors had no clear way forward.

03

The Solution

The redesign answers each problem with one clear change.

01

A message that lands fast

A visitor should understand the product in the first few seconds. The new hero says it plainly: attendance, payroll and HR in one place.

02

Content that is easy to scan

Features, benefits and pricing needed clear groups, so people can find what matters to them without reading the whole page.

03

One obvious next step

Book a demo, start a free trial or get the app: each action is clear, and repeated where a visitor is ready to act.

04

Page Structure

The page follows the questions a business owner asks, in the order they ask them.

What is it? Who trusts it? What can it do? Why switch? What does it cost? What do other customers say? What if I still have questions? Each section below answers one of these, and the page ends with a way forward.

  1. HeroMessage and main call to action
  2. Client logosEarly proof
  3. FeaturesPeople, Workforce, Business
  4. BenefitsWhy teams switch
  5. PricingModules and bundles
  6. TestimonialsReal customer words
  7. FAQsAnswers before the demo
  8. Final call to actionBook a demo or start a trial
05

Homepage

The hero now says what the product does and offers three clear next steps.

  • A

    Hero message

    The headline, “Automate HR and Empower your Business”, is followed by one line that names payroll, attendance, recruitment and employee management in one cloud platform.

  • B

    Feature tabs

    Features are grouped under People, Workforce and Business, so each visitor can go straight to what they care about. Every card has three short points and a Learn more link.

  • C

    Calls to action

    Book a demo, Start free trial and Get the app sit together in the hero, then return in the middle of the page and at the end.

06

Pricing

Pricing is per employee and depends on which modules a business picks, so the page had to make that easy to understand.

Businesses can choose between paying as they use and a bundled plan, switch modules on or off, and watch the total for their team size update straight away. When one module needs another, the dependent ones switch on with it, and settings, payments and billing are always included. A yearly toggle shows the 15% saving.

07

Features

Features are grouped under People, Workforce and Business, so each visitor can go straight to what matters to them.
